Executive Summary

Todd MacDonald is a senior AI strategy advisor and organizational transformation architect with more than three decades of experience helping enterprises, academic institutions, and government bodies navigate complex technology transitions. He specializes in Human-Centered Applied AI—cultivating the mindsets, skills, and innovative thinking needed to translate frontier AI research into durable real-world progress.

Using a unique combination of leadership psychology and applied technology, Todd serves as a trusted thought partner to C-suite executives, provosts, deans, and board members who need more than a vendor pitch — they need a seasoned guide who has stood in their shoes and delivered results.

Core Value Proposition

Todd bridges the gap between AI's theoretical promise and organizational reality. His engagements deliver:

  • Strategic clarity on where AI creates durable value versus where it introduces unacceptable risk
  • Practical roadmaps that align AI initiatives with institutional strategy, governance, and culture
  • Independent, vendor-neutral counsel - no commercial technology or platform bias
  • Accelerated readiness by building internal AI literacy, champions, and governance structures
  • Credibility with both technical teams and senior leadership, enabling faster alignment and execution

Typical Engagements

Todd's work typically takes one of the following forms:

Strategic Advisory Retainer

Ongoing, confidential access for executives navigating AI decisions in real time. Monthly sessions, ad hoc counsel, and participation in key leadership discussions.

AI Readiness Assessment

A structured diagnostic evaluating an organization's AI maturity across strategy, data, talent, governance, and culture — delivered as an actionable executive report with prioritized recommendations.

Board & Executive Education

Custom workshops and briefings designed to bring senior leaders from AI-curious to AI-confident. Topics range from foundational literacy to governance, risk, and competitive positioning.

AI Strategy Sprint

An intensive 60–90 day engagement to develop or stress-test an AI strategy, build internal alignment, and produce a board-ready implementation roadmap.

Academic Program & Curriculum Advisory

Consulting for colleges and universities seeking to integrate AI across disciplines, build applied AI programs, or reposition institutional identity around AI leadership.
