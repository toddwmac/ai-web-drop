Executive Summary

Todd MacDonald is a senior AI strategy advisor and organizational transformation architect with more than three decades of experience helping enterprises, academic institutions, and government bodies navigate complex technology transitions. He specializes in applied artificial intelligence: translating frontier research into practical, revenue-generating, and mission-critical deployments.

Using a unique combination of leadership psychology and applied technology, Todd serves as a trusted thought partner to C-suite executives, provosts, deans, and board members who need more than a vendor pitch — they need a seasoned guide who has stood in their shoes and delivered results.

Core Value Proposition

Todd bridges the gap between AI's theoretical promise and organizational reality. His engagements deliver:

  • Strategic clarity on where AI creates durable value versus where it introduces unacceptable risk
  • Practical roadmaps that align AI initiatives with institutional strategy, governance, and culture
  • Independent, vendor-neutral counsel — no commercial technology or platform bias
  • Accelerated readiness by building internal AI literacy, champions, and governance structures
  • Credibility with both technical teams and senior leadership, enabling faster alignment and execution
